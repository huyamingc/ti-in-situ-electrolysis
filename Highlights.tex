\singlespacing

\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.45em}
  \item An electrochemical design basis is established for molten-salt titanium production
  \item Magnesium and calcium are co-deposited as regenerable reductants in one cell
  \item A 75--147 mV co-deposition window survives activity-coefficient uncertainty
  \item Mass-transport analysis sets the operating current density and electrode gap
  \item Net energy of 12{,}552 kWh per ton of titanium requires current efficiency above 0.70
\end{itemize}
