Electrochemical Design Basis for Titanium Production by Mg-Ca Co-Deposition in a Quaternary Chloride Melt

Yaming Hu

- An electrochemical design basis is established for molten-salt titanium production
- Magnesium and calcium are co-deposited as regenerable reductants in one cell
- A 75-147 mV co-deposition window survives activity-coefficient uncertainty
- Mass-transport analysis sets the operating current density and electrode gap
- Net energy of 12,552 kWh per ton of titanium requires current efficiency above 0.70
